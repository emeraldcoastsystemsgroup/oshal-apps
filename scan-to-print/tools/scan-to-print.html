        On a P2S or an H2 printer, Settings → Settings → LAN Only shows its address (IP) and the 8-character Access Code; other Bambu Lab printers show both on their LAN Only (LAN mode) settings page. The printer's serial and model are read from the printer itself. Jobs are sliced for it here and sent over your home network. To let oshal start prints, not just upload them, turn on LAN Only and then Developer Mode on that same page.
