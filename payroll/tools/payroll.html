Direct deposit (ACH)

oshal generates the NACHA file; your own bank executes it. No money moves through oshal and no third party is involved. Send a zero-dollar prenote first if your bank wants accounts validated before a live run.

Tax forms

Worksheets carrying the exact numbered line values. oshal does not transmit to the IRS or SSA — you or your filing service transcribe these onto the return.

Electronic W-2 filing (SSA EFW2)

Builds the machine-readable file you upload to SSA Business Services Online. Run it through AccuWage Online first — AccuWage checks the format, not whether names and Social Security numbers match SSA records. Producing the file decrypts every SSN, so it asks you to confirm. The layout is verified per tax year and an unverified year is refused rather than guessed: tax year 2026 adds Box 12 codes TT and TP, which have no field in the 2025 layout.

Did everyone actually get paid?

Approving a run says who should be paid. A deposit can still come back days later — R02 account closed, R03 no account — and until that return is imported a failed deposit looks exactly like a successful one.

Import returns and notifications of change

Paste the return file your bank sends back. A return means the money came back and that person is unpaid. A notification of change means the opposite — the payment succeeded and the bank is correcting the details for next time.

Print checks

Allocates check numbers from your sequence — a number, once issued, is never reused. No MICR line is generated. Print onto your bank's pre-encoded check stock: the standard that governs the MICR band is not public, the available vendor documentation contradicts itself on field positions, and a line one position out is rejected by the bank's reader or posted to the wrong account.

Banking calendar

Two calendars, because they disagree. The Federal Reserve decides whether payroll funds; the IRS decides when a deposit is due. A holiday on a Saturday costs the Fed nothing but still moves the IRS deadline, and DC Emancipation Day moves the deadline while the banks stay open.
