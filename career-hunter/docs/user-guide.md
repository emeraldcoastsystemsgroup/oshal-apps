# Career user guide

This guide describes the Career 1.60.10 experience for a job seeker. Start on **Home**.
Your installation and assigned role determine which pages are available. On a
phone, use the bottom bar and its menu to reach the rest of Career; your role is
shown in the menu.

Career helps you find openings, prepare an application and keep its history.
Your résumé, saved jobs and application records belong to your signed-in account.
For account setup and the current limits of coaching support, see the
[deployment guide](deployment.md).

## Start your search

1. Open **My résumé** and upload your résumé. Wait for Career to finish reading
   it; an upload or indexing error needs attention before tailored work can run.
2. Open **Settings → What I'm looking for**. Review your job titles, matching area and remote
   preference. A matching area uses a US city and state or ZIP and a radius.
3. Open **Find jobs** to search the collected public openings. You can do this
   before adding a résumé. Open **My jobs → New matches** for the separate list
   of actionable matches against your résumé.

**My résumé** holds your main résumé and tailored copies. **Other experience**
holds projects, work, volunteer activities and skills you can choose for a
particular application. Saving an Other experience entry leaves the main résumé
unchanged. **Improve my résumé** helps you record examples from your own work;
keep the facts and outcomes accurate.

## Ask the Concierge

Career has one assistant, the **Concierge**. Press **Concierge** in Career's bar on any page. It is the same assistant
on every page and in one conversation: what you said on Work history is still there on My jobs. It opens beside the
page on a computer and over it on a phone, where **Back to** the page's name returns you to the page. Jarvis hands a
Career request to this same Concierge.

Ask it for anything you do in Career, from whatever page you are on:

- your jobs: "what are my best matches", "list my saved jobs", "save this job", "take it off my list", "I applied to
  it yesterday", "prepare an application for it";
- your résumé and work history: "add a job", "change the end month of my last job", "tighten the second bullet",
  "is my new job in my résumé?";
- your settings: "only match remote jobs", "look for platform engineer jobs within 50 miles of Destin";
- the companies you want and your recruiters: "add Acme's careers page", "mark the recruiter at Acme as contacted".

It reads your saved Career data and makes the change itself. A change to your résumé or work history, and anything
that spends your AI allowance or goes to an employer, appears as a card in the conversation: press **Approve** to
apply it or **Cancel** to leave things as they are. A résumé change shows on the page at once with **Undo**. If you
approve two cards one after the other, each is applied to your résumé as it is at that moment. A card is refused only
when something it names changed after it was written; the Concierge then tells you which change and which job, and
you ask again.

## One résumé

Your **main résumé** is one record. **Work history** shows its jobs, each with its title, employer, months and bullets.
**My résumé** shows the same record as a Word document you can type in. They are the same thing, not two copies:

- a job or a bullet you add on Work history, or approve on a card anywhere in Career, is in the document the next
  time you open My résumé, with no extra step;
- what you type in the document is saved into your main résumé when you press **Save to main résumé**, before the
  Concierge answers you on that page, and when you next open My résumé or Work history;
- if you typed in the document and something else changed your work history meanwhile, both are kept.

If a document was made before this version and Career cannot tell whether it or your main résumé is newer, My résumé
says so and leaves it alone: **Use my work history** puts your main résumé in the document, and **Save to main résumé**
keeps what is typed in it. The earlier text stays in the document's Revision history either way.

Ask "Is my résumé loaded?" to check your signed-in account. If none is saved, open **My résumé** to upload one. An
unreadable record is reported as unavailable, so it does not mean you have no résumé.

## Your expanded experience

Client work and projects behind your jobs are your **expanded experience**, and they are on **Work history** (Résumé,
then Work history). The line under its heading counts your jobs, client engagements and client accounts; press one to
go to it.

- **Client engagements**: each client or project you worked on, with your role, the industry, its months or length,
  the technology and bullets. One you place under a job shows on that job's card. Those not placed yet are under
  **Expanded experience**, **Not placed under a job yet**: choose the job in **Place all under** and press **Place
  them** to put them all under the job you did them in (for example a consulting job).
- Press **Edit** on an engagement to change it, to choose **Done under** (its job), to add bullets, or to **Move up**,
  **Move down** or **Remove this engagement**. **Add an engagement** adds a new one.
- **Client accounts**: the companies and agencies you worked with, by group. **Edit** a group to change its names, one
  per line; **Add a group** starts another.
- Every change shows **Undo**. **Download my data** lists all of it too.

Career scores jobs against your whole main résumé, which can be at most 128 KB; a change that would make it larger
says so and is not saved.

## Begin with Today

Home's **Today** section links to work that needs attention, such as an
application reply, a match to review, a follow-up or interview preparation. Open
the step to continue with the relevant job. Items depend on what Career has
actually read and which pages you can open.

**Where everything stands** shows your saved and application stages separately
from New matches. New matches counts the current actionable match set; it is not
the number scored in the past day. A dated scoring activity count describes work
done during that period. A missing count or failed read is not evidence of zero
jobs.

## Find and save jobs

Enter a title, skill or employer in **Find jobs**. Open **Filters** for advanced
title and description fields, company, place, pay, arrangement and posting age.
Choosing a place and radius narrows the results before they are paged. Map pins
and distances use approximate recorded locations and straight-line distance.

**Most relevant** puts titles containing every searched keyword first, followed
by newest first-seen time. With no keywords it uses newest first. This is a text
ordering, not a résumé fit assessment. You can choose another sort; an explicit
or saved choice remains selected. If the page reports a **limited search
sample**, older description matches may be omitted. Narrow the search to explore
further.

A fresh phone search starts with a smaller result batch. The range shown is the
returned page, not a total for the whole search. Use the page controls for more.
**Saved searches** keeps successful criteria on your account, including chosen
filters and sort. A failed refresh does not prove that there are no results.

Open an employer posting to check its current requirements and availability.
Pay estimates are labelled; **About this pay estimate** explains their source.
An estimate is not an employer's promised pay.

Choose **Save** to keep a job in **My jobs → Saved**. Wait for the **Saved**
acknowledgement. With saved-job preparation off (the default), Save starts no fit
check or document generation.

To request scoring, open **My jobs → Saved**, open the job and choose
**Check my fit**. **Not scored yet** means Career has no saved AI fit for the job.
A genuine running check shows its progress; a failed check reports an error.
A score is decision support, not a hiring prediction or guarantee.

In **New matches**, use **Save** or **Pass** on the phone card. The list view uses
**Not interested** for the dismiss action. These choices do not send an
application. Saved jobs stay separate from matching eligibility, so changing
your matching area does not erase work you deliberately saved.

## Choose saved-job preparation

In **Settings → Check for new jobs now → Saved-job preparation**, turn on
**Prepare saved jobs for me** and press **Save preparation choice**. It starts
preparation for your existing saved jobs and future saves, using your main résumé
and each job's saved tailoring and experience selections. A job with no Other
experience choice of its own uses the one you prepared your last application with;
until you have chosen once, Career asks first: the job waits, and the saved line says
how many wait. This setting belongs to your own account and starts off. Choose:

- **Review each application first**: Career prepares the documents, then holds the
  application for you. Read its résumé and cover letter, then choose **Review
  complete · ready to submit**. Changed inputs or document bytes need a fresh review.
- **Let Career finish preparation by itself**: Career generates the documents and
  marks the application **Ready to submit** without that extra review press.

Both choices stop before sending. **Ready to submit** includes a link to the
employer's page; Career does not send the application to an employer automatically.
Apply on the employer's form, or separately approve the exact packet for your
connected computer to send as described below. This choice does not turn on bulk
submission or change any existing submission approval.

Open **My jobs → Getting ready** for progress, review and anything stuck. A stopped
job gives its reason; correct it and retry generation in its application. Career
does not retry failures or uncertain work by itself. If an attempt is pending but
no longer progressing, use **Retry interrupted preparation**. An active account
writer can refuse that retry until the current work ends. Turning preparation off
stops future preparations; work already started may finish. Changing the choice
applies to new preparation attempts; existing attempts keep their recorded choice.
A job removed before preparation is queued is left alone.

## Prepare one application

Open a saved job and choose **Prepare application**, or use **Open
application** for work already started. The application stays with that job
through these steps. Prepare opens the tailoring workspace; choose Generate explicitly
when you want documents with saved-job preparation off:

1. **Tailor** — review the main résumé, add notes and select relevant Other
   experience. Choose **Generate résumé & cover letter** when ready. Career never
   generates a résumé before you have seen this step. The **Other experience ·
   optional** boxes are ticked as in the last application you prepared (the line under
   the heading says so); change them for this job, and Generate uses exactly the boxes
   ticked when you press it. A job whose documents were made before you chose opens on
   this step with a notice; **Review generated documents** opens them as they are.
   For a job with no description, this step starts with the box **Paste the job
   description from the posting** and **Open the posting** beside it. Open the
   employer's posting, copy the whole job description and paste it in the box.
   When Career knows it cannot read the job (it says **Job details needed**, also
   after a preparation that stopped because Career could not read the job),
   **Generate résumé & cover letter** (or **Retry generation**) is greyed out and
   stays off until you have pasted at least 200 characters; the line under the box
   counts them and says why. Career keeps what you paste as
   your own copy of that job's description, for you only (up to 20,000 characters),
   and uses it to check your fit and write your résumé and cover letter. To change
   it later, open **Replace the description you pasted** in the same step, paste
   the new description and generate again. Jarvis and the Concierge cannot prepare
   such a job until its description is pasted here; they tell you so, in the same
   words: open **Prepare application**, step **1 · Tailor**, paste the job
   description from the posting and generate there.
2. **Review documents** — read the actual generated documents and download or
   edit them as needed. If the résumé, tailoring or selected experience changes,
   generate a current packet before sending it.
3. **Send** — choose how to apply.

Preparation and a generated document are not submission receipts. If preparation
fails, read the error and retry after correcting its cause. Your retained notes
and earlier documents remain available where the page says they were kept. If Career
cannot confirm the preparation write, choose **Check preparation** to read its
saved state. If nothing was recorded, a separate **Prepare application** press
tries again. If the workspace did not open, **Open application** tries opening
it again without repeating the acknowledged preparation.

To prepare several jobs at once, open **My jobs**, switch to **Table**, tick the jobs
and press **Prepare applications**. Career asks once, before anything starts: the
same **Other experience · optional** boxes under **Include in every selected
application**, and the number of applications. **Cancel** starts nothing; **Prepare**
saves your choice on each job and prepares them one or two at a time while My jobs is
open.

### Apply yourself

Choose **Open the employer's form** and use the downloaded résumé and cover
letter. After completing the employer's process, return and choose **I applied**.
Career records this as your own report. It has no independent confirmation that
you sent the application. If you pressed it by mistake and the correction is
available, open **Pressed I applied by mistake? → Return to preparation**.

### Have Career send from your computer

**Send it for me from my computer** is available only when the dedicated Career
submission computer is connected. **How to set up your computer** explains that
connection; an administrator can help with the
[submission worker setup](../NATIVE-SUBMISSION.md).

Review the employer, destination, exact documents and applicant details before
**Approve & submit**. The approval applies to that packet and job. Unanswered
employer questions before a form press return for your answers and another review.

A field for both documents receives the résumé only. Career records that the cover
letter had no place to go; it never substitutes the cover letter for the résumé.
Separate résumé and cover-letter fields receive their respective approved files.

A stop without employer questions shows the reason. Choose **Review application
again** after fixing it, **Apply yourself instead** and then **I applied** when you
finish by hand, or leave it for later. Reviewing does not send anything; another
attempt needs **Approve & submit** for its exact packet.

**Check the submission outcome** means Career cannot safely try again. Check the
employer's application history or receipt email. The page shows its kept browser
picture when available; the picture alone is not proof of receipt. Then choose:

- **It was sent**: Career records your word as Applied and closes the attempt.
  Another automatic attempt remains blocked.
- **It was not sent**: Career closes the attempt. You must review and approve a
  new packet before trying again. This button sends nothing.

You may leave the outcome undecided. Career keeps both your statement and the
picture, when available. Older attempts may have no picture. Native sync cannot
reopen an outcome you closed.

The approval screen says: “This form can have more than one step; Career will press
up to 4 buttons on jobs.example.com.” It shows the employer's actual site. That
number is part of the packet you approve. Career asks permission for each press,
never presses the same form step twice, and stops at an unfamiliar page or a page
that tries to leave the approved site. Continue can itself send an application;
a visible receipt after Continue counts as confirmation. Reaching four presses
stops the attempt without pressing a fifth button.

The job page and Submission activity show each button's words, time and result,
with a picture when one was kept. A large picture is reduced before its verified
fingerprint is recorded. If permission was given but the computer did not report
back, Career says that whether it pressed the button is unknown. It never retries
a lost permission request. A lost picture-report reply is retried once with the
same report.

If Career only opened the form with Apply and then found a missing answer, you
can answer and review again. After Continue or Submit, an unconfirmed outcome
stays held for you to check. A receipt is the point where Career records Sent.

The computer helper must be reinstalled from 1.60.10 before using new approvals;
older helpers refuse their changed packet. Ask the person who set it up to update
it. At each run the helper clears only this employer's cookies in its own browser
profile. Your usual browser and other sites' cookies stay as they were.

A queued, started or failed attempt does not establish that an application was
sent. **Resume approved submission**, when offered, continues the retained
approval; it is not a reason to submit a second copy yourself.

## Record interviews, offers and closed applications

Open **Applications**, choose the job and open **View application**. In
**Application status → Update this application**, use:

| What happened | Action |
|---|---|
| An interview was arranged | **I have an interview**, enter **Interview date and time**, then **Save interview** |
| You received an offer | **I got an offer** |
| The application ended | **It closed** |

The interview field uses the time zone shown beside it. Career saves the instant
and displays it in the viewing device's local time. The page says when you
recorded an outcome yourself. A refused save keeps the form available for
correction; check the saved status before leaving.

The stages are **Getting ready**, **Applied**, **Interviewing**, **Offer** and
**Closed**, alongside your Saved and New matches lists. Open **Prepare for
interview** from the application's status to review the role and your supporting
experience. The **Job market** page keeps your own progress separate from shared
market counts; its Getting ready number uses the same preparation count.

## Connect Gmail and review replies

Open **Settings → Accounts → Connect Gmail** or **Reconnect Gmail**. Finish the
native sign-in window, then read the saved authorization state. Use **Refresh
account status** if the window did not update it. **Stop waiting** stops this
page's wait; it does not revoke an authorization already saved.

Saved Gmail authorization and mailbox access are different facts. The Accounts
section separately shows the dated last successful mailbox check and last
attempt. The mailbox used for application updates must match the submission
email in your résumé. Connecting alone starts no mailbox check and sends no
message.

Use **Settings → Email updates** for the monitor's existing enable, pause and
check controls. The monitor can attach a sufficiently clear employer reply to an
application and record its outcome; an ambiguous message is not an automatic
outcome update. Open a reply
from its job to inspect the original email. A connection or check failure needs
attention; it does not establish that nobody replied. Manually recorded outcomes
remain distinguishable from email evidence and submission confirmation.

## Set a pace that suits you

Home offers an optional **Application goal for the past 7 days**. Set a whole
number from 1 to 100 with **Save goal**, change it later or use **Turn off goal**.
This is a rolling seven-day period, not a calendar week or a recommendation to
send more applications.

Progress counts recorded applications in that period, including ones that later
reached an interview, offer or closed status. It does not mean every application
was independently verified as sent. A successful goal save is separate from the
fresh progress read; if progress cannot be read, use **Check progress again**.
Career reports attainment only from the count it actually read.

## Keep the rest of your search together

**Recruiters** keeps the firms and people you add, their status and your next
action. **LinkedIn profile** helps prepare profile changes for your own signed-in
browser. Its update result must say what actually happened; approving a draft
does not itself update LinkedIn. Optional Portrait Studio and Social pages appear
only when your installation and role admit them. Social account authorization
does not verify a LinkedIn browser session.

**Download my data** shows the recorded profile, experience, supporting history
and answers, with a Markdown download. Review the contents before sharing them.
This page is not a grant for another person to access your workspace.

For help, give your support contact the page name, action, time and visible error.
Include the job or run reference only through your installation's appropriate
support channel. Ask them to check the recorded outcome before repeating an
uncertain send. The [deployment guide](deployment.md) describes operator checks
and current coaching limits.

If Career says it is working on your account, your refused draft edits stay on
screen and are not saved yet. Wait for the work to finish, then choose
**Retry saving draft**. A job **Save** refused with this message stays available
for another press; Career does not retry it automatically. Reload a saved draft
only when Career reports an actual revision conflict.

Saved open jobs offer **Remove**. The row disappears as soon as you press it. If Career
cannot save the removal, the row returns with a reason and you can retry. Removed
jobs stay in **Removed** and the nightly match pass does not offer the same posting
again. Preparing one from Removed is an explicit choice to bring it back; saved
drafts and application records are retained. Recorded applications keep **Unsave**,
which takes them out of Saved while keeping them in Applications.

Nightly matching works through jobs that have not yet been scored, including jobs
missed by an earlier failed pass. Existing matches stay available while it runs.
Use **Check my fit** when you want a new assessment of a particular job.
A job whose listing has no description, and whose employer page Career cannot read
on this installation, is left without a match score and does not hold up the others.
Since 1.66.0 such a job still reaches **New matches** when one of your saved job titles
is in its title, with what Career knows: title, employer, location, posted date and a
link to the employer's posting. It reads **Details needed** where its fit would be,
with "Career could not read this job's details. Open the posting and paste its
description in Prepare application to get your fit and a tailored résumé." and
**Open the posting**. It has no match score until you give its description, and
**Check my fit** is not offered for it. Under **Best AI fit** these jobs come after
every scored match; under **Date posted** they sit by date among the others. The
nightly pass lists up to 200 new ones a night, those most like your saved titles and
your résumé's keywords first.
Career reads the full description of Workday, Oracle and SmartRecruiters jobs, so
those are scored. Since 1.64.0 the nightly job search also keeps the description of
new jobs from Google, Eightfold, Experis, Radancy, SuccessFactors, iCIMS, Avature and
most employers' own career sites when it finds them, so those are scored too: a new
job usually has its description by the next morning, and since 1.64.1 the jobs found in
the days before are caught up within about three nights. Some employers' sites do not let
Career read a job's page (for example Jacobs, CBRE and Delta); their jobs stay without a
match score.

Where your installation has turned on **settled scoring**, the nightly pass scores many more
jobs each night (its administrator sets how many and for how long). Each job is asked three
times; a score is kept only when the three answers agree which side of 70 the job is on and
are not close to 70. The jobs most like your saved titles go first. In **My jobs**:

- **Not scored yet**: the pass has not reached the job yet. It is not a low score; the job
  is scored on a later night, first among the jobs left.
- **Close call, not settled yet**: the three answers disagreed, or landed close to 70.
  The job is not counted as a strong match. It is asked again the next night; **Check my
  fit** settles it now.

**Settings → Nightly résumé matches** says what the last night did: how many jobs were scored,
how many close calls are not settled yet, how many could not be read, and how many were
not reached yet.
