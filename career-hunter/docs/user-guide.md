# Career user guide

This guide describes the Career 1.60.8 experience for a job seeker. Start on **Home**.
Your installation and assigned role determine which pages are available. On a
phone, use the bottom bar and its menu to reach the rest of Career; your role is
shown in the menu.

Career helps you find openings, prepare an application and keep its history.
Your résumé, saved jobs and application records belong to your signed-in account.
For account setup and the current limits of coaching support, see the
[deployment guide](deployment.md).

## Start your search

1. Open **My résumé** and upload your résumé. Wait for Career to finish reading
   it; an upload or indexing error needs attention before tailored work can run.
2. Open **Settings → What I'm looking for**. Review your job titles, matching area and remote
   preference. A matching area uses a US city and state or ZIP and a radius.
3. Open **Find jobs** to search the collected public openings. You can do this
   before adding a résumé. Open **My jobs → New matches** for the separate list
   of actionable matches against your résumé.

**My résumé** holds your main résumé and tailored copies. **Other experience**
holds projects, work, volunteer activities and skills you can choose for a
particular application. Saving an Other experience entry leaves the main résumé
unchanged. **Improve my résumé** helps you record examples from your own work;
keep the facts and outcomes accurate.

## Ask the Concierge

Use **Concierge** in Career's bar to ask about your saved résumé, jobs or applications.
It uses the same conversation panel as the document editor: beside the page on a
computer and above the bottom bar on a phone. **Close concierge** returns to the
page; reopening keeps the conversation while you stay on that page.

Ask “Is my résumé loaded?” to check your signed-in account. The answer reads your
saved main résumé and its section headings, without loading its full text into
the conversation. If none is saved, open **My résumé** to upload one. An unreadable
record is reported as unavailable, so it does not mean you have no résumé.

Career holds the saved main résumé used for matching and tailoring. **My résumé**
opens a separate **Master resume (Career)** Word document in AI Office. Edits made
there reach Career when you use **Save to main résumé**. The Concierge beside that
document edits it with approval cards; ask Career for a section's wording when you
want to discuss saved text.

## Begin with Today

Home's **Today** section links to work that needs attention, such as an
application reply, a match to review, a follow-up or interview preparation. Open
the step to continue with the relevant job. Items depend on what Career has
actually read and which pages you can open.

**Where everything stands** shows your saved and application stages separately
from New matches. New matches counts the current actionable match set; it is not
the number scored in the past day. A dated scoring activity count describes work
done during that period. A missing count or failed read is not evidence of zero
jobs.

## Find and save jobs

Enter a title, skill or employer in **Find jobs**. Open **Filters** for advanced
title and description fields, company, place, pay, arrangement and posting age.
Choosing a place and radius narrows the results before they are paged. Map pins
and distances use approximate recorded locations and straight-line distance.

**Most relevant** puts titles containing every searched keyword first, followed
by newest first-seen time. With no keywords it uses newest first. This is a text
ordering, not a résumé fit assessment. You can choose another sort; an explicit
or saved choice remains selected. If the page reports a **limited search
sample**, older description matches may be omitted. Narrow the search to explore
further.

A fresh phone search starts with a smaller result batch. The range shown is the
returned page, not a total for the whole search. Use the page controls for more.
**Saved searches** keeps successful criteria on your account, including chosen
filters and sort. A failed refresh does not prove that there are no results.

Open an employer posting to check its current requirements and availability.
Pay estimates are labelled; **About this pay estimate** explains their source.
An estimate is not an employer's promised pay.

Choose **Save** to keep a job in **My jobs → Saved**. Wait for the **Saved**
acknowledgement. With saved-job preparation off (the default), Save starts no fit
check or document generation.

To request scoring, open **My jobs → Saved**, open the job and choose
**Check my fit**. **Not scored yet** means Career has no saved AI fit for the job.
A genuine running check shows its progress; a failed check reports an error.
A score is decision support, not a hiring prediction or guarantee.

In **New matches**, use **Save** or **Pass** on the phone card. The list view uses
**Not interested** for the dismiss action. These choices do not send an
application. Saved jobs stay separate from matching eligibility, so changing
your matching area does not erase work you deliberately saved.

## Choose saved-job preparation

In **Settings → Check for new jobs now → Saved-job preparation**, turn on
**Prepare saved jobs for me** and press **Save preparation choice**. It starts
preparation for your existing saved jobs and future saves, using your main résumé
and each job's saved tailoring and experience selections. This setting belongs to
your own account and starts off. Choose:

- **Review each application first**: Career prepares the documents, then holds the
  application for you. Read its résumé and cover letter, then choose **Review
  complete · ready to submit**. Changed inputs or document bytes need a fresh review.
- **Let Career finish preparation by itself**: Career generates the documents and
  marks the application **Ready to submit** without that extra review press.

Both choices stop before sending. **Ready to submit** includes a link to the
employer's page; Career does not send the application to an employer automatically.
Apply on the employer's form, or separately approve the exact packet for your
connected computer to send as described below. This choice does not turn on bulk
submission or change any existing submission approval.

Open **My jobs → Getting ready** for progress, review and anything stuck. A stopped
job gives its reason; correct it and retry generation in its application. Career
does not retry failures or uncertain work by itself. If an attempt is pending but
no longer progressing, use **Retry interrupted preparation**. An active account
writer can refuse that retry until the current work ends. Turning preparation off
stops future preparations; work already started may finish. Changing the choice
applies to new preparation attempts; existing attempts keep their recorded choice.
A job removed before preparation is queued is left alone.

## Prepare one application

Open a saved job and choose **Prepare application**, or use **Open
application** for work already started. The application stays with that job
through these steps. Prepare opens the tailoring workspace; choose Generate explicitly
when you want documents with saved-job preparation off:

1. **Tailor** — review the main résumé, add notes and select relevant Other
   experience. Choose **Generate résumé & cover letter** when ready.
2. **Review documents** — read the actual generated documents and download or
   edit them as needed. If the résumé, tailoring or selected experience changes,
   generate a current packet before sending it.
3. **Send** — choose how to apply.

Preparation and a generated document are not submission receipts. If preparation
fails, read the error and retry after correcting its cause. Your retained notes
and earlier documents remain available where the page says they were kept. If Career
cannot confirm the preparation write, choose **Check preparation** to read its
saved state. If nothing was recorded, a separate **Prepare application** press
tries again. If the workspace did not open, **Open application** tries opening
it again without repeating the acknowledged preparation.

### Apply yourself

Choose **Open the employer's form** and use the downloaded résumé and cover
letter. After completing the employer's process, return and choose **I applied**.
Career records this as your own report. It has no independent confirmation that
you sent the application. If you pressed it by mistake and the correction is
available, open **Pressed I applied by mistake? → Return to preparation**.

### Have Career send from your computer

**Send it for me from my computer** is available only when the dedicated Career
submission computer is connected. **How to set up your computer** explains that
connection; an administrator can help with the
[submission worker setup](../NATIVE-SUBMISSION.md).

Review the employer, destination, exact documents and applicant details before
**Approve & submit**. The approval applies to that packet and job. Unanswered
employer questions return for your answers and another review.

If the outcome is unknown, check the employer's application history and the
retained submission activity before authorizing another attempt. A queued,
started or failed attempt does not establish that an application was sent.
**Resume approved submission**, when offered, continues the retained approval;
it is not a reason to submit a second copy yourself.

## Record interviews, offers and closed applications

Open **Applications**, choose the job and open **View application**. In
**Application status → Update this application**, use:

| What happened | Action |
|---|---|
| An interview was arranged | **I have an interview**, enter **Interview date and time**, then **Save interview** |
| You received an offer | **I got an offer** |
| The application ended | **It closed** |

The interview field uses the time zone shown beside it. Career saves the instant
and displays it in the viewing device's local time. The page says when you
recorded an outcome yourself. A refused save keeps the form available for
correction; check the saved status before leaving.

The stages are **Getting ready**, **Applied**, **Interviewing**, **Offer** and
**Closed**, alongside your Saved and New matches lists. Open **Prepare for
interview** from the application's status to review the role and your supporting
experience. The **Job market** page keeps your own progress separate from shared
market counts; its Getting ready number uses the same preparation count.

## Connect Gmail and review replies

Open **Settings → Accounts → Connect Gmail** or **Reconnect Gmail**. Finish the
native sign-in window, then read the saved authorization state. Use **Refresh
account status** if the window did not update it. **Stop waiting** stops this
page's wait; it does not revoke an authorization already saved.

Saved Gmail authorization and mailbox access are different facts. The Accounts
section separately shows the dated last successful mailbox check and last
attempt. The mailbox used for application updates must match the submission
email in your résumé. Connecting alone starts no mailbox check and sends no
message.

Use **Settings → Email updates** for the monitor's existing enable, pause and
check controls. The monitor can attach a sufficiently clear employer reply to an
application and record its outcome; an ambiguous message is not an automatic
outcome update. Open a reply
from its job to inspect the original email. A connection or check failure needs
attention; it does not establish that nobody replied. Manually recorded outcomes
remain distinguishable from email evidence and submission confirmation.

## Set a pace that suits you

Home offers an optional **Application goal for the past 7 days**. Set a whole
number from 1 to 100 with **Save goal**, change it later or use **Turn off goal**.
This is a rolling seven-day period, not a calendar week or a recommendation to
send more applications.

Progress counts recorded applications in that period, including ones that later
reached an interview, offer or closed status. It does not mean every application
was independently verified as sent. A successful goal save is separate from the
fresh progress read; if progress cannot be read, use **Check progress again**.
Career reports attainment only from the count it actually read.

## Keep the rest of your search together

**Recruiters** keeps the firms and people you add, their status and your next
action. **LinkedIn profile** helps prepare profile changes for your own signed-in
browser. Its update result must say what actually happened; approving a draft
does not itself update LinkedIn. Optional Portrait Studio and Social pages appear
only when your installation and role admit them. Social account authorization
does not verify a LinkedIn browser session.

**Download my data** shows the recorded profile, experience, supporting history
and answers, with a Markdown download. Review the contents before sharing them.
This page is not a grant for another person to access your workspace.

For help, give your support contact the page name, action, time and visible error.
Include the job or run reference only through your installation's appropriate
support channel. Ask them to check the recorded outcome before repeating an
uncertain send. The [deployment guide](deployment.md) describes operator checks
and current coaching limits.

If Career says it is working on your account, your refused draft edits stay on
screen and are not saved yet. Wait for the work to finish, then choose
**Retry saving draft**. A job **Save** refused with this message stays available
for another press; Career does not retry it automatically. Reload a saved draft
only when Career reports an actual revision conflict.
