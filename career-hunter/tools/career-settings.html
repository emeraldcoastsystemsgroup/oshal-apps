Daily digest

Once a day, OSHAL checks for new high-fit matches and sends them to you over your own connected channel — email from your Gmail, or a text from your Twilio. On by default; nothing is sent on days with no new matches, and never more than once a day.

Automation

Off unless you turn it on. With automation off, OSHAL never generates application drafts or queues submissions on its own — the nightly run leaves your queue untouched, and everything happens only when you click it. Opting in to auto-generate lets the nightly run AI-score fresh matches and add draft applications to your Approvals queue (you still approve each one). Auto-submit is a separate outward-action consent: it authorizes connected desktop workers to fill employer forms and click their final Submit buttons for ready applications in bulk, without a separate per-job click. CAPTCHA and two-factor prompts still pause for you.

Add career materials

Upload more than a résumé — work samples, exported job emails, a LinkedIn “Download your data” archive, or a status report. OSHAL reads them and adds what you actually do to your profile. Add-only: nothing already in your profile is overwritten.

Remote only

When this is on, the automated match considers only postings the feed flags as remote: the nightly AI scoring pass skips on-site roles (so no tokens are spent on jobs you would not take), your digest carries remote roles only, and your job board defaults to remote. The Job Search screen keeps its own Any / Remote / On-site pills, and an explicit pill always wins.

Target titles

On top of the keyword prefilter, OSHAL runs a daily title pass that AI-scores roles whose titles match your target list — so your best-fit roles surface even when the keyword score misses them. It considers jobs posted in the last 7 days (or first seen this week when the feed has no posted date). Seeded from your uploaded resume; edit any time (comma-separated). Each run is capped, and it runs at most once a day.

Matching area

Local and hybrid jobs must be within this radius. Include remote jobs to consider them independently of office distance; matching still checks location eligibility. Distances use city coordinates, not driving routes.

Nightly resume matches

After each evening's job refresh, this job matches recent openings to your resume, then scores up to 50 new or changed jobs using your titles and matching area. It checks for published results every 30 minutes and runs once per evening refresh cycle. Enable, pause or run it below.

Application email updates

Check your connected submission mailbox every 15 minutes for acknowledgements, interviews, offers and rejections that match an application on your board. This reads email; it sends no messages or job applications. Enable it again here if an application update pauses the routine for review.

Target companies (your list)

The shared company list is the portal's — an admin curates it under Companies. This is your extension of it: paste an employer's careers or jobs URL and OSHAL adds that employer to the nightly scrape. A URL is accepted only when it matches a supported job board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Workday, iCIMS, Eightfold and the other boards the engine reads); anything else is rejected and not stored.
