Refresh

Pull the latest jobs into the shared corpus and keyword-index them, then AI-score your fresh in-lane roles. (Nightly + midday runs do this automatically.)

Daily digest

Once a day, OSHAL checks for new high-fit matches and sends them to you over your own connected channel — email from your Gmail, or a text from your Twilio. On by default; nothing is sent on days with no new matches, and never more than once a day.

Automation

Off unless you turn it on. With automation off, OSHAL never generates application drafts or queues submissions on its own — the nightly run leaves your queue untouched, and everything happens only when you click it. Opting in to auto-generate lets the nightly run AI-score fresh matches and add draft applications to your Approvals queue (you still approve each one). Auto-submit additionally allows bulk queueing of ready applications for desktop submission without a per-job click.

Add career materials

Upload more than a résumé — work samples, exported job emails, a LinkedIn “Download your data” archive, or a status report. OSHAL reads them and adds what you actually do to your profile. Add-only: nothing already in your profile is overwritten.

Target titles

On top of the keyword prefilter, OSHAL runs a daily title pass that AI-scores roles whose titles match your target list — so your best-fit roles surface even when the keyword score misses them. Seeded from your uploaded resume; edit any time (comma-separated). Each run is capped, and it runs at most once a day.
