Answers you give up front

Attestations

Some employers ask you to agree to their terms and to sign their self-identification form. Career does these for you only when you switch them on here, only on a form you approved, and always in your name.

Written questions

Some employers ask a question you answer in your own words, such as “What platform have you built from scratch?”. Career drafts an answer from your record and the cover letter it wrote for that job, and lists it on Answers to review.

How Career fills in a form

Like a person is the default. It is slower, and it changes nothing inside the employer’s page: Career only reads the page, and your computer’s own mouse and keyboard do every click, key and paste, as you would. Fast is quicker, but the browser add-on then sets each value inside the page, which a site can tell from a person.
