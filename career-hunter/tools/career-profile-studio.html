How Career updates LinkedIn

Make your LinkedIn read like a professional site: headline, about, skills, custom URL, a background banner, and your résumé in Featured.

LinkedIn gives applications no way to edit a profile, so Career makes the changes in your own Chrome, on your computer, after you press Update LinkedIn. Nothing changes on LinkedIn until you have pressed both Approve and Update LinkedIn, and Career never posts to your feed or notifies your network.
