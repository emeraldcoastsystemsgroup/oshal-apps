# Ambient Recall (person-model)

Ambient Recall (`person-model`) gives each signed-in person an opt-in, owner-private surface over their own ambient transcripts on oshal: exact mention counts with verbatim quoted receipts, assistant-voice playback without storing audio or cloning voices, open asks and commitments paired with their source quotes, per-person consent controls, and weekly topic trends.
