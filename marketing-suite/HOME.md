# Marketing on Home

The application group composes its active member summaries and its readiness steps: Marketing
Engine, Switchboard, Social, Brand Graphics, AI Office and Video. It owns no counter of its own and
must not duplicate a member's totals or add unlike numbers together. Its declared toolbar and setup
dependencies remain the source of activation readiness. Disabled members stay disabled; the group
never arms a channel, publishes an item, or creates a campaign to make a Home card appear.
