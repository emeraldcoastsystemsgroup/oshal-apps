# Movies Home and connected planning

`watchlist-titles` counts the caller's saved `movies_watchlist` rows with status `want`, excluding future creation dates. The three newest saved titles lead the card. This is a watchlist, not a claim about viewing activity. `asOf` is the read time; each entry retains its saved time.

Selected watchlist context can prepare a meal or music discussion. The native screen also offers a ride-planning draft. Incoming `planning-brief` context opens an editable panel even when TMDB is disconnected. “Use in concierge” copies it after connection; Send stays explicit. No title search, streaming, ticket purchase or watchlist change occurs on Home GET or context receipt.

Optional partners: Eats, Spotify, Rides. TMDB remains the existing provider prerequisite for discovery. Missing tables are unavailable, not an empty watchlist. Compiled route boundaries, actual PostgreSQL owner isolation and native browser handoffs are covered by the household test scripts.
