# Create on Home

Create 1.5.0 combines its private manual image projects with its member studios' saved work. Create owns `/api/create/home-summary`, backed only by the current verified issuer and subject's project records. The installed summary declaration makes that evidence available to the default Home dashboard. Image cards reopen the exact project in `create-editor` with a validated ID.

AI Office, Portrait Studio, Video Studio, LoRA Studio, Vids Studio, Creative Studio, 3D Scan-to-Print and Brand Graphics retain their own records and labeled counts. Create reads those existing probes in the viewer's session and does not sum unlike metrics. Access is checked before a member is probed. Missing or failed data remains unavailable; it is not displayed as zero.

The Image editor uses the imported application permissions and private record scope described in [EDITOR.md](EDITOR.md). Opening the launcher does not grant generation, render, camera, upload or printer permission in another app. The 1.4.0 Scan-to-Print entry continues to open the owning application unchanged.

## The Home screen (UX audit 2026-11-06)

Home opens on **Continue creating**: your work from every studio as picture cards, newest first, two columns on a phone and 160px columns on a desktop. A saved Create image is drawn by the image editor's own renderer from your own project (`GET /api/create/projects/<id>`, read only once Create access is confirmed). The member studios' summaries carry no picture, so their cards are a tinted cover with the studio glyph and the title. Each card's meta is in words ("Image editor · 2 days ago", "Built · revision 1", "Reconstructed from photos", "Saved PowerPoint"). Skeleton cards hold the grid while a studio is still answering. A tap on a card opens it; its ⋯ holds Open, then Rename, Duplicate and Delete as greyed "Not available yet" rows (backlog RP-CRE-01).

One **New** button sits at the bottom right. It opens the New sheet on a phone and a dialog on a desktop; this is the former New screen, and `/api/create/new` now forwards to `/api/create/home?new=1` (keeping a known `?cat=`). The sheet starts with a Blank row, each opening a blank document in the studio that owns it: Image (the editor's blank canvas), Presentation, Document and Spreadsheet (AI Office `kind=pptx|docx|xlsx`), Video (the video editor's empty timeline), Portrait, Story and 3D scan (each studio's first step). Custom size (RP-CRE-02) and Import a file (RP-CRE-03) are greyed planned controls. Templates follow, with a search field, one sideways row of format chips and "No templates match" with Clear; image designs come before the Office formats, and the Office templates still come from AI Office's own starter catalog.

Search sits in the app row on a desktop and behind a search icon on a phone, and covers both your work and the templates ("1 match", "2 matches"). "Set up your brand" is one row above the work, shown only while you have no brand kit; the Brand Kit rail tile edits a saved kit. Home is Create's first surface, so it also serves the family and company audience view the shells frame with `?audience=`.
