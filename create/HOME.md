# Create on Home

Final disposition: compact creative launcher. Create owns no records or counters. Its member
studios — AI Office, Portrait Studio, Video Studio, LoRA Studio, Vids Studio and Creative
Studio — supply saved-work evidence and counts in their own Home cards and suite highlights,
and the Create home surface reads those same member probes in the viewer's session rather
than summing unlike metrics. Create declares no `summary:` of its own, so its Home tile is
built from its `Create: …` jarvis_tasks rows. A disabled member stays disabled; loading Create
does not authorize a provider call, a render or a generation.
