## What is actually being manufactured

Everything in the box: inflatable envelope with a translucent projection panel, blower, short-throw projector, compute module running the face locally, speaker, mic, power distribution, stakes. Plug into one outdoor GFCI outlet and it works.

**The physical form factor is not being invented.** This is the most valuable competitive fact in the file, and it is good news. It establishes that a projector inside a cold-air inflatable, outdoors, for a full Halloween season, at a 99.99 USD RETAIL price, is a solved manufacturing problem. The geometry, the thermal behaviour, the weather tolerance and the retail acceptance are all demonstrated by a shipping product. This venture is not inventing the physical form factor - it is putting a different light engine inside a proven one.

What is being changed is the light engine. The gap is not a feature Gemmy has declined to add - it is a different product architecture. A rotating gobo has no frame buffer, no audio pipeline and no compute, so no amount of iteration on that light unit produces a face that talks. Closing the gap means adding a video engine, a renderer, an audio path and a network stack - which is the BOM difference between their 99.99 USD unit and this venture's V-A.

In manufacturing terms that means the venture is not developing an inflatable — it is specifying one, adding a purpose-made translucent panel to it, and integrating a projector, a compute module, an audio path and a power harness into a housing that has to survive a night outdoors. **The inflatable is the solved part. The integration is the venture.**

## Lead time, and the reason it is the binding constraint

Component production lead time is registered at 10 weeks with a 6–14 week range, and supplier qualification at 7 weeks with a 3–10 week range. **Neither is in the dataset.** Both were minted by this run because the engine cannot schedule a production order without them, and both are flagged accordingly.

That matters more than it looks. At the high corner of both ranges the critical path becomes 24 weeks, and the engine **blocks** the scenario: goods reach the warehouse in 2027-09 against a window that opens in 2027-08. A seasonal product that arrives after Halloween has not made a smaller profit; it has made no revenue and holds a full run of inventory with near-zero residual value.

**Two of the eight questions this plan cannot answer are lead times nobody has stated.** A single conversation with one contract manufacturer would replace both.

## Quality and yield

No yield figure, no first-pass-yield target, no inspection plan and no acceptable quality limit exists anywhere in this dataset. The model carries a scrap rate of 2.5% with a 0.0%–5.0% range purely so the arithmetic has something to work with, and that range appears in the sensitivity chart as $28,141 of movement in the peak cash requirement.

For a product whose failure mode is condensation on an optical surface after six unattended hours outdoors, the absence of any inspection plan is not a documentation gap. It is the gap.
