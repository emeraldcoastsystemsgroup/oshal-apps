## The request-for-quotation package

Three quotations on the projector line are the highest-leverage action available to this venture, and a badly-written request produces a number that is worse than none because it looks like evidence.

**Specify the projector in measured ANSI lumens with a test report, and refuse anything else.** Vendors in this class routinely quote marketing 'lumens' 3 to 5x above measured ANSI lumens. A 600-lumen listing is commonly a ~150 ANSI unit. Any quote MUST be specified and accepted in ANSI lumens with a test report, or the whole photometric model is wrong.

**State the throw ratio as a requirement, not a preference.** 150 to 400 ANSI lumens (measured, with test report), 720p minimum, throw ratio 0.5 to 0.8, HDMI in, LED light engine with 20000 h rated life, keystone correction

**Ask every supplier for the four things the model is missing**, because each of them is registered here as a guess: the minimum order quantity, the production lead time, the qualification and first-article time, and the assembly or integration charge if it is quoted separately from components.

**Ask what the price is at 500 units as well as at 5,000.** The dataset's own small-run uplift of 25%–60% is an estimate, and the pilot economics turn on it.

## Single-source exposure

Every component in this plan has exactly one source, because every component has zero sources. Two are worth naming separately:

- **The projector.** It is the largest line, the top of the sensitivity chart, and the one whose required specification may not exist in its assumed price class at all. A second source is not a resilience measure here; it is the primary evidence-gathering exercise.
- **The compute module.** Low end is the OEM Android TV stick class; high end is a Raspberry Pi CM5 (published 45 to 95 USD retail single-unit, S23) which buys long-term supply and a real Linux. The choice is a supply-security vs cost tradeoff, not just a price tradeoff.

A seasonal product has one shipment a year and no chance to recover from a supplier failure inside the season. The dataset's schedule places the last usable purchase-order date at 2027-03; after that a supplier problem does not delay the season, it removes it.
