How the gate works. Every hand shown on the Scan tab is recorded as a prediction and graded when the market settles. A strategy is UNPROVEN until it has enough settled predictions, then FAILING if its Brier score is no better than the market's — either way its suggested stake is forced to 0%. Only a PROVEN strategy (one that beats the market's Brier) is allowed to size a real bet. Optimism has to be earned, not assumed.
