Forward-test scorecard — a strategy may only stake real money once it has BEATEN the market's Brier score on settled predictions

StrategyStatusGradedPendingOur BrierMarket BrierHit rateP&L/contract
No strategy has made a prediction yet. Once the scan or the weather model records one, it appears here and is graded at settlement.

How the gate works. Every market with an edge on the Scan tab is recorded as a prediction and graded when the market settles. Grading reads Kalshi's public results, so it runs with or without a Kalshi account: opening this tab grades what is waiting, and the background scan job grades between scans. A strategy is UNPROVEN until it has enough settled predictions, then FAILING if its Brier score is no better than the market's — either way its suggested stake is forced to 0%. Only a PROVEN strategy (one that beats the market's Brier) is allowed to size a real bet. Optimism has to be earned, not assumed.

What the books mean. Paper · auto is the ledger, sized: every strategy's every pick replayed at the quarter-Kelly stake recorded with it, against a paper bankroll, graded when the market settles — the record the Scorecard rules on. It is paper money throughout; no order is ever placed from it, and a pick registered at zero stake moves the bankroll not at all. Paper · manual is you, on the demo exchange. Real · manual is you, on the live exchange, confirm-gated. Real · auto does not exist: an autopilot needs a PROVEN strategy first. Days to verdict is how long, at the observed grading pace, until a forward test has the 30 settled picks the Scorecard needs.

Alert history — each market is announced ONCE, the first time it clears your floor

Nothing yet. When the background scan finds a market with an edge that clears your alert floor, it lands in your Jarvis feed and is listed here.

Alerts are first-seen: the scan re-finds the same market every cycle, so re-announcing it would train you to ignore it. Outcome is the settlement grade of the pick exactly as announced — WIN or LOSS, and the P&L of one contract bought at the alerted ask, fees included. Since announced is the same market's price move while it is still OPEN: the ask you were quoted versus the latest candle on the same side, in cents, green when the market moved toward the pick. It is read from Kalshi's public candles, which are shared and rate-limited, so a busy refresh prices the newest alerts first and says below the table how many it left for the next one. It is a paper record (an alert never places an order) and the same grades feed the Scorecard tab. Change the floor, the strength, or the daily cap on the Settings tab. Jarvis gets these in his feed — ask him "what did the Kalshi scan find?" and he answers from what actually happened, not a fresh scan.

Help: reading Kalshi

Big edge
The scan's strongest rating: our estimate beats the price by the most after the fee, with the most settled history behind it.
Good edge
A clear edge after the fee, with less behind it than a big one.
Small edge
The smallest edge the scan still counts: at least 1.2¢ a contract after the fee, with 35% confidence.
No edge
After the fee the price is about right, or too high, for what history supports, so the scan passes.
No estimate
The scan does not price it: it closes more than 48 hours out, it is a parlay leg, one side has no price, or it closes within minutes.
Buy · Yes 54¢ · No 47¢
What one contract of each side costs now, the ask. A contract pays $1 if its side wins, nothing if it loses.
Our estimate
The chance that the side wins, from how often Kalshi markets at that price and in that category settled that way (the model data, dated on the line under the account).
Edge
Our estimate minus the price and Kalshi's fee, in cents a contract. Plus means the side is cheaper than history says it should be.
Paper trading
Nothing here places a real order. Every pick is recorded on paper and graded when its market settles: that is the Scorecard. With your Kalshi account connected you can also place your own orders, one at a time, after a confirmation.

The Scan table

True P = calibrated probability from Kalshi's own settled history (beta-shrunk toward price; n = observations behind it). Edge (net) = True P − (ask + taker fee). Stake = quarter-Kelly, halved per risk flag, capped at 5% of bankroll. Orders are limit-only and confirm-gated; the LIVE exchange is refused unless explicitly enabled. Stake is forced to 0% until a strategy out-scores the market on settled predictions: the pick is still shown, it just hasn't earned a slice of the bankroll. Every pick there is recorded as a prediction and graded at settlement. The table is a snapshot kept warm by your background-scan job (turned on from the Jobs screen) on the cadence in Settings; opening this page never waits on a scan.
