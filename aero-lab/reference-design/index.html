Verification summary

  • STL: wing.stl 576 triangles, bbox [208.0, 2500.0, 25.31] mm vs expected span 2500 / chord 208 / max thickness 24.98 — watertight (every edge on exactly 2 triangles) for all three meshes.
  • DXF (airfoil/ribs): re-parsed; template = 1 closed 399-vertex polyline spanning exactly 208.0 mm; ribs.dxf = 8 polylines + 8 spar-hole circles; web at 30% chord 24.98 mm vs 10 mm hole.
  • Gore: re-parsed (2 closed polylines, 1052 vertices); net length 3192 mm, max net width 300 mm; 10 × net gore area matches the spheroid surface to -0.000 %.
Open item (honest): vent/ballonet is not designed. Sealed in day sun the hull would reach ~+9.1 kPa (≈81 MPa hoop in 38 µm film — failure); at night superpressure goes to −1.5 kPa (slack hull). See BUILD_SHEET.md. Pack option B (125.5 Wh) is a fast-trim result sized for a larger hull; in this hull it means f = 0.73 and the equinox day was not re-certified.
