# Life on Home

Life is a compact household launcher, not a separate activity ledger. Movies, Spotify, Eats, Rides, Shopping, Travel, Payments and Bake-Off own their saved work and next actions. Their metrics appear under their declared Home suites; Life is not declared as a core application group and does not claim to aggregate them into its own tile. Keep the launcher configurable and default on without adding duplicate or incompatible totals.
