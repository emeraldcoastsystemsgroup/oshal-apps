# Little Monsters — User Guide

How to use **Little Monsters**, the study companion that makes schoolwork easier to read, easier
to remember, and a little more fun. Written for students first, with a short section for teachers
at the end. As-built as of 2026-09-27 (package 1.4.4).

> Other guides: [Installation](installation.md) · [Support & FAQ](support.md) · [School deployment](school-deployment.md) · [All docs](README.md)

---

## Getting in

Open the app link your school gave you and **sign in with your school account** (the same
Microsoft account you use for email and class work). The first time you sign in, your monster is
created for you — no separate password to remember. After that, the app opens straight to your
**Home**.

A quick welcome walks you through the basics the first time. You can skip it and come back to
anything later.

Little Monsters looks the same wherever you open it: from the cockpit ribbon, inside the
**Little Monsters · classroom** experience (where every tool opens in place), or on its own page,
where a top bar carries the same tools. It wears the playful classroom look by default and follows
the style you choose in the cockpit or the experience picker.

## Your home page

Home is your daily base. What it shows depends on who you are:

**Students** see

- **Your level and XP.** XP is the points you earn for studying. Fill the bar and you **level up**;
  every level drops a mystery box, and Home tells you when one is waiting.
- **Cards reviewed, quiz average and lectures** for your classes. A **day streak** tile appears
  once you have a streak going.
- **What would you like to do?** — the tutor, flashcards, a practice quiz, My Day, the games and
  My Monsters, one tap each.
- **Coming up** — assignments due soon, with the date.
- **My classes** — tap a class to open it. Open classes your teachers have published show above
  the list so you can join with one tap; **Class bank** lists them all, and **Create a class** makes
  a private study class just for you.

**Teachers** see the classes they teach with student counts, lecture counts and quiz averages,
quick actions to record a lecture, open the Teacher view, create a class, build flashcards or
replay a presentation, and the same class list and class bank.

The first time you land on Home **after leveling up**, you get a little confetti party — and if
you've earned a mystery box you haven't opened, a button to go open it.

## My Day — your schedule in one place

**My Day** gathers everything on your plate from all your classes: what is due today and what is
coming up, a strip of this week, your classes, and a month calendar **color-coded by class**, with
a legend so you can tell them apart at a glance.

Two things stay on the device you are using: the **study timer** (it counts the minutes the page
is open) and your **daily reflection** notes. They are not shared and do not follow you to another
computer.

## The tutor — your Little Monster Expert

The friendly monster bubble floating on the screen is your **tutor**. Tap it any time to:

- **Ask for help** with a question you're stuck on.
- **Check your homework** — show it your work and it tells you where to look again.
- **Add a photo or use your camera.** Tap the camera to point it at a worksheet or a page in your
  book; tap the upload button to send a picture or file you already have. The tutor can read it
  and help.
- **Have it read aloud** — handy if reading is the hard part.

Open the tutor from inside a class and it answers from that class's approved material and your own
uploads. The tutor is built to **coach, not cheat**. It won't just hand you the answer — it walks
you through it, gives you a similar practice problem, or asks questions that get you there yourself.
That's on purpose, so the learning actually sticks.

## Flashcards

Everything for flashcards lives in **one place**. From the Flashcards tab you can:

- **Browse** the sets you and your classes already have.
- **Study** a set. For each card you tap **Got it** or **Don't got it** — the app spaces the ones
  you miss so they come back more often until you know them.
- **Create** your own set: type cards yourself or **paste** a list (one `term: definition` per
  line), then **edit** any card right where you find it.

Sets **made from class material** (the textbook, a lecture) are built by your teacher and appear
in the class's set list; students do not see the generate buttons because the server only accepts
those requests from a teacher or admin.

Studying flashcards earns XP.

---

## Practice quizzes

A practice quiz is always about **one class**, so the Quiz page first asks which class, then builds
a short quiz from that class's material and grades it on the server. Your results are private to
you and count towards your quiz average on Home.

---

## Lectures — recorded by teachers, replayed by everyone

**Teachers** use the **Record** tab during class: live captions appear while they speak, and when
they stop, Little Monsters turns the lecture into notes, slides, flashcards and any assignments it
heard. The recording is uploaded so the replay and the study material can be made.

**Students** do not record — the Record tab (like the Teacher view) is not offered to them in any rail, because the app tells the cockpit which tools each signed-in person may see. Recent lectures show on Home
and in each class with **Replay** (the slides), **Cards** (the flashcard set) and **Listen** (the
audio) as soon as they are ready. **Presentations** replays any lecture as slides and can export
them.

## The toolkit

- **Formula Lab** — a reference shelf of physics and chemistry formulas. Tap a formula for a
  **worked example**, or send it to the tutor.
- **STEM Helpers** — a scientific calculator, plus links that open a periodic table, physics and
  chemistry simulations and a graphing calculator in a new tab.
- **Citations** — add the sources you used and it builds your **Works Cited** list (MLA or APA)
  for you to copy in.
- **Timelines** — add dates and events and watch them line up in order.
- **My Files** — quick notes you keep here, saved to or opened from your computer.

Citations, timelines and My Files are kept **in this browser only**; they are not uploaded and do
not follow you to another device. Cloud storage connections are made in the hub, not from this page.

---

## The arcade — games that count

The **Games** tab is a six-game arcade: Snake, Monster Dash, 2048, Flashcard Blitz, Quiz Arena,
and Milkshake Maker. They work with both **keyboard and touch**, so they play on a laptop or a
tablet.

- **A quick warm-up first.** Before a game starts you answer a few flashcards from your class.
  Study, then play.
- **Your score earns XP**, so playing well moves your level bar. Best scores stay on this device.
- **When you're done, you're in charge.** Every game-over screen gives you **Play again** or
  **I'm done** — "I'm done" takes you back to the arcade.

## Rewards & My Monsters

This is the fun part, and **you have to earn it** — there are no free boxes.

1. **Level up** by earning XP (studying, flashcards, quizzes, games).
2. Each level you gain grants a **mystery box**.
3. Open a box by **picking one of three** — inside is either a **new monster** or an **accessory**
   (a hat, glasses, hair, and more).
4. Everything you collect is kept in **My Monsters**. **Equip** any monster or accessory to dress
   up your avatar there.
5. Your monster even **learns new tricks** (little animations) as your collection grows.

## What's private and what's shared

- **Private to you:** your XP, level, flashcard progress, quiz results, and your monster
  collection. No other student can see these.
- **Kept on this device only:** the study timer, reflections, citations, timelines, My Files notes,
  arcade best scores and your read-aloud voice settings.
- **Shared with your class:** the class's textbook, lectures, flashcard sets, slides, quizzes,
  assignments, and calendar — visible to everyone **in that class**.

If a class isn't yours, you won't see it, and you can't reach another student's private progress.

## Filing a file into a class

When an image or PDF is open in the cockpit, choose **Send to… → File into a class**. Little
Monsters shows the classes available to your signed-in account; select one and confirm **Import**.
The server verifies the owner-bound handoff and class access before creating the material, so the
file is never accepted for a class chosen by an untrusted caller.

Package 1.4.4 binds this hand-off. An installation still running an earlier package, for example
because its administrator has not yet migrated role assignments to the 1.4.4 access catalog, refuses
the import with "authorization_operation_unbound". Until then, upload the file from the class page
instead.

Teachers and administrators see the material as approved immediately. A student can file material
for an accessible class too, but it remains a teacher-review request until approved. Use **Ask the
Tutor about it** when you want a context-free pending attachment instead of class material.

## Voice

**Voice** (at the end of the tool list) chooses how read-aloud sounds: on or off, which voice, and
how fast. The choice is saved on this device and applies across Little Monsters. The page also
shows which voice engines the server has available.

## For teachers

You set up the learning; students bring the studying. The essentials:

- **Create a class** and **publish** it so students find it in the class bank; **enroll** students
  from the class page (they appear after their first sign-in).
- **Upload the class textbook (PDF).** This is what grounds the tutor, the flashcards you generate,
  and the quizzes for that class — so they stay on *your* material. Students can file material for
  review; you approve or deny it from the class page.
- **Record lectures** from the Record tab; recording, transcript processing and flashcard
  generation from class material are teacher and admin actions.
- **Teacher view** shows every class you teach with its roster, quiz averages, cards reviewed and
  last activity per student.
- Only the teacher of a class edits, publishes, archives or deletes it and changes its roster.

The how-to for sign-in, class creation, enrollment, and textbook upload is in the
[School Deployment Guide](school-deployment.md). If something isn't working, see
[Support & FAQ](support.md).
