Original sources

Select text, images or shapes on the page to edit their properties. Double-click text to type in place. PDF text stays in its own object; it does not reflow like Word.

Drawn or uploaded signatures are visual page placements saved in your personal store, not cryptographic or certified digital signatures. Encrypted or certificate-signed files, XFA, active actions and redaction remain unsupported. A highlight or ink mark does not remove content.

Converted Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx) and PowerPoint (.pptx) files reconstruct paragraphs, detected tables and embedded images approximately; complex multi-column layouts may shift. Single-page, two-page and night mode change reading display only and leave exported PDFs unchanged.

Up to 4 MiB of sources and 32 pages per document. An original used by saved history stays retained.
