Create a circuit, open one, or start from an example. Click a part in the palette to add it, drag it into place, click one pin then another to wire them, then Run.

Drag a part from the palette onto the canvas (or click it) · drag to move · drag on empty canvas to select several, then move or Delete them together · click pin → pin to wire · drag a wire's middle segment to re-route it · double-click a wire to add a bend, drag a bend to move it, double-click it to remove it · click a wire to plot its net · Delete removes · R rotates (a selection turns about its centre) · Esc cancels · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo · Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy / paste · wheel zooms · shift-drag pans

Drag a part to another hole · click a part and press R to turn it (upright, its legs down the rows; two legs on one strip short it, and the board says so) · click one hole then another to add a jumper — drawn straight at its real length, click it to read the mm · click a jumper and press Delete to remove it · the five holes of a column are one strip, a rail is one strip · the schematic's wires are rebuilt from the board after every edit (gears and loads stay on the schematic)
