# Calendar Preparation

The core Calendar is a workflow schedule view, and jarvis-overview calendar is empty; neither is a personal-event source. This package owns explicit primary-Google-calendar sync into a minimized caller-owned snapshot, protected by owner RLS. It preserves the existing core view. No sync runs on GET. Missing snapshot is Not synced, never zero. A saved snapshot contains at most 250 events in the next 30 days; counts are saved events, not a complete agenda. All-day dates retain their original date. Source sync time stays visible. Gift, meal, travel and meeting preparation use optional versioned app receivers. No attendees or descriptions are transferred, no occasion is inferred, and no booking or purchase is triggered. Destination apps own discussion, research and provider deep links. The caller connects Google through Identity and explicitly syncs; there is no deployment-token fallback.
