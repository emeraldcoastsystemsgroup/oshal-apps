# ADR 0016: TriageNorth is the product; the platform is a dependency

Status: Product/foundation boundary directed by the operator, 20 October 2026. TriageNorth is the proposed product name; submission documents updated.

This supersedes the product naming and submission framing of ADR0001, while retaining
its shared-framework architecture and stable `intelligent-operations` identifiers.

TriageNorth is the working customer-facing name for a secure AI analysis and
response hub. Event-to-Action is the initial domain workflow. OSHAL is the existing platform foundation providing security, workflows, UX, connections and tickets,
installed as a versioned Docker/OCI baseline before the product package. Disclose
inherited platform capabilities rather than claiming them as hackathon inventions.

The submission emphasizes operational interpretation, explainable filtering/reduction,
temporal correlation, bounded evidence, the investigation experience and controlled
response. Generic means reusable across supported evidence sources and analysis
workflows; it does not mean unrestricted agents or automatically supported connectors.

The commercial positioning centers on application value and optional deployment/support
services. Foundation commercial arrangements remain outside this application decision.
MARKET-RESEARCH.md documents competitive overlap,
public prices and limited cost examples from primary sources; it records hypotheses
and differentiates current behavior from predictive and execution roadmap gates.

Acceptance: product-led brief/proposal, explicit foundation boundary in editable Visio,
research sources and assumptions, updated plan and exports. Stable runtime IDs and
historical acceptance receipts are preserved; changing submission language does not
claim a deployed UI rename or new functional capabilities.

Foundation attribution follows the existing project licensing reference:
AGPL-3.0-or-later with a paid commercial alternative. Native-kernel metadata conflicts
with that reference and remains a baseline publication check, not a new application
license decision. See MARKET-RESEARCH.md for source provenance.
