Context that survives the alert storm
Three rolling telemetry banks preserve accepted observations for at least 15 minutes after receipt. A full ring applies backpressure rather than deleting fresh context. A matched capture reservation can pin a bounded, preprocessed packet outside the ring.
Map a named stream
OTLP JSON: POST /api/intelligent-operations/streams/SOURCE_ID/v1/logs or /v1/metrics. CloudWatch: /streams/SOURCE_ID/cloudwatch accepts decoded subscription JSON. Use an authorized collector to decompress AWS transport and convert syslog or file logs to OTLP; these endpoints use your existing account authority.
Anomaly detection
A local statistical detector scores each new metric sample against the baseline it learned from earlier samples of the same resource and metric, before learning from it. Candidates are admitted as alerts under the source you choose, so they pass the same stored-query filtering and noise reduction as any source alert. Cold, stale and late samples are explicit; nothing imported is counted as a detection. The pinned dataset baselines and the NAB pilot receipt record measured behaviour.
